### Technical report - Complex Systems Design, MSc Computer Science (AI), University of Camerino

Piece P14 of the AI Puzzle framework (consolidated version 5.0). Assignment of the piece is provisional (OPEN_QUESTIONS Q1).

## 1. Introduction and AI Puzzle context
The AI Puzzle framework describes 26 autonomous AI projects for public and private organizations. Each piece must be
"useful on its own, while also preparing it so that its outputs can become inputs to other pieces"; connections are
"composition opportunities", not dependencies [General architecture and puzzle rule]. P14 belongs to the area
*Documents, Knowledge & Organizational Memory* and sits low in the functional stack: it turns documents into verifiable
evidence that other pieces (P12 Generation, P15 Meetings, P24 External assistant, P25 Expert knowledge) can consume.
This makes two properties central: **provenance** (every output must be traceable to a source) and **interfaces**
(other pieces need a stable contract and persistent identifiers). Course requirements verified against the source
document are in COURSE_REQUIREMENTS.md.

## 2. Problem and objectives
The P14 sheet asks for "a search engine capable of finding information by meaning, not only by exact keyword match",
across heterogeneous documents, that "always return[s] the precise references to the sources". Objectives: reduce search
time, retrieve content despite different wording, connect sources on the same topic, keep provenance verifiable.
Our prototype addresses them for a fictional public body, **Ente Demo**, whose knowledge is the Italian legislation
it must apply plus its own internal documents. Out of scope: crawling a real intranet or mailboxes (no real
organization; privacy), and any generative answer (the summary is extractive).

## 4. Architecture and design decisions
One FastAPI service, one Qdrant container, one SQLite file (no microservices). Diagrams: docs/diagrams/
(system context, components, data model, classes, ingestion, search, shadow mode, integration).

Main decisions (DECISIONS.md):
- **Hybrid retrieval with rank fusion.** BM25 (SQLite FTS5, ADR-004) captures exact legal terms and article numbers;
  a small multilingual embedding model (ADR-005) captures meaning and cross-lingual matches. Reciprocal Rank Fusion
  uses ranks only, so no score normalisation is needed.
- **ACL inside retrieval.** One rule set produces both a SQL clause and a Qdrant payload filter; unauthorised passages
  never enter ranking. Results are re-checked at hydration (defence in depth). Unauthorised documents return 404.
- **Human approval gate** (ADR-007): ingestion parses and chunks; only an owner's approval indexes.
- **Persistent identifiers**: `document_id` from the source, `document_version` = content hash, `passage_id` = hash of
  (document, version, chunker version, offset); superseded versions stay resolvable.
- **Evidence, not confidence**: scores are exposed with per-retriever ranks; `insufficient_evidence` uses thresholds
  calibrated on a tuning split.
- **Robust, safe parsing** (ADR-006): magic-number type checks, size limits, macro/XXE/zip-bomb DOCX rejection,
  pdfium + pypdf for difficult GU fonts, a document-order HTML walk for Normattiva markup.

## 5. Data
Case study (ADR-003, DATA.md): 32 real Italian official acts downloaded from Normattiva and the Gazzetta Ufficiale
(not covered by copyright, art. 5 L. 633/1941, verified on the official text) and 25 **synthetic, LLM-generated**
internal documents of Ente Demo with public/internal/restricted levels. 57 documents, 10,689 passages after chunking
(`eval/results/corpus_stats.json`). D2: BEIR SciFact (CC BY 4.0 / ODC-By 1.0) as a sanity benchmark.
Data quality was a real issue: amendment markup, fonts without Unicode maps, unrelated acts and personal data inside GU
issues (DATA.md, OPEN_QUESTIONS Q11).

chosen on the tuning split only.

Findings, to be read with the limitations in section 10:
- Hybrid (S2) clearly beats the BM25 baseline (randomization test in EVALUATION.md); on D1 it is close to dense-only S1,
  helping mostly conceptual queries and recall; on D2 SciFact it is the best of the three, as expected.
- Paraphrased questions in everyday Italian are the hard case: BM25 largely fails there, dense retrieval helps but
  vocabulary mismatch with legal language remains the main error category.
- English questions are answered from Italian documents by the multilingual model (BM25 cannot), but the
  insufficient-evidence threshold, calibrated on mostly Italian tuning queries, is too strict for them.
- Target T2 (correct source in the top 3 for at least 80% of queries) is **not met**. The error analysis attributes the
  misses to vocabulary mismatch, right-document-wrong-section, and incomplete judgments.

## 8. Integration
REST/OpenAPI contract frozen in `contracts/`, with JSON Schemas and real examples; drift is caught by tests.
Two connections are implemented as independent mock consumers: **P24** (public-only key; answers only from cited
passages; escalates to a human on insufficient evidence) and **P12** (builds a draft where every statement carries a
`passage_id`, re-resolved before use). P11, P15 and P25 are documented as producers of `IngestDocument`.
Observed with the live system: P24 escalates correctly on unanswerable questions, but for a question about a
restricted budget it answered from an unrelated public passage - the ACL held, the evidence check did not. This is
the case for human escalation and a reranker.

## 9. Security, traceability, human oversight, validation before automation
SECURITY_OVERSIGHT.md. In short: API keys per consumer (secrets only in `.env`), ACL inside retrieval with 0 leaks in
the evaluation, safe parsing, RFC 9457 errors without internals; every response carries model, chunker, pipeline and
index-snapshot versions; every query is logged with a pseudonymised principal; feedback records the operator's
decision. Human oversight points: owner approval before indexing, user judgement on cited passages, explicit
`insufficient_evidence`, extractive summary. Shadow mode is implemented (`DISPLAY_SYSTEM`/`SHADOW_SYSTEM`,
`shadow_log`); a real shadow phase with real users was not possible and is simulated by the offline evaluation.

## 10. Limitations
- No real organization: internal documents are synthetic and LLM-written; access levels of public laws are assigned by us.
- Queries and judgments were drafted by an LLM (the same that wrote the synthetic documents) and are incomplete; the
  student must review them (ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md).
- Small test set (60 in-scope, 10 out-of-scope queries); thresholds calibrated on 6 out-of-scope tuning queries.
- Source-quality rubric and time-to-information were not measured (no human evaluation).
- No Italian stemming in BM25; some GU PDF pages remain noisy; GU issues include unrelated acts and personal data.
- API-key authentication only; no identity provider; latency measured in-process on one machine.
- The extractive summary can include off-topic sentences.

## 11. Future work
Cross-encoder reranker (S3) and per-language threshold calibration; pooled human judgments by independent annotators;
a real shadow phase with users; `/related` endpoint and CloudEvents `p14.document.indexed`; MCP wrapper for LLM
consumers (P12, P24); removal of non-target acts from GU issues; tombstones (410) and /v2 policy implementation.
