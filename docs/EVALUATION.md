# Evaluation (deliverable 4)

## Research questions
- RQ1: Does hybrid retrieval (dense + BM25, S2) outperform a BM25-only baseline (B0) on our question set?
- RQ2: Does a cross-encoder reranker improve early precision enough to justify its latency? - **not answered** (reranker is a stretch goal, not implemented).
- RQ3: How does performance vary by query type (keyword, paraphrase, conceptual, out-of-scope)?
- RQ4: How do the systems behave on English queries over an Italian corpus (cross-lingual)?

## Systems compared
B0 BM25 only (SQLite FTS5, ADR-004) - S1 dense only (multilingual-e5-small, ADR-005) - S2 hybrid (RRF, k=60, 50 candidates per retriever). S3 (hybrid + reranker) not implemented.

## Data and protocol
- D1 case study: 57 documents, 10,689 passages (docs/DATA.md). Question set `eval/queries/d1_queries.jsonl`: 110 queries, **drafted by an LLM** (`llm_generated: true`), split into 40 tuning and 70 test queries; the test split was frozen (SHA-256 in `eval/queries/test_freeze.json`, commit `b5748af`) before the embedding-model choice and the threshold calibration. Annotation protocol: docs/ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md.
- Each query is run with a principal that can see its target documents (e.g. `hr-officer` for restricted HR documents); retrieval depth 10 passages.
- D2 sanity check: BEIR SciFact (English), independent qrels, document-level metrics.

## Operational definitions
- Qrels at **passage level**, graded 0/1/2 (grade 2 = contains the verbatim answer snippet; grade 1 = same document and section; see the protocol).
- **Recall@K, Precision@K, MRR@10** use binary relevance = grade 2. **nDCG@10** uses graded gains (2^g - 1).
- **Correct-source rate** (correct_source@3): share of in-scope queries for which at least one of the top-3 passages has grade >= 1, i.e. points to a target document AND the right section.
- **Source quality** (manual 3-level rubric): NOT measured - requires human judgment; listed in the student review checklist.
- **Abstention correctness**: share of out-of-scope queries flagged `insufficient_evidence`. **False abstention rate**: share of in-scope queries flagged.
- **ACL leakage**: returned passages whose document the principal may not see, checked with an oracle re-stated independently in `eval/run_eval.py`, for all 9 principals x 70 test queries x 3 systems.
- **Latency**: end-to-end POST /v1/search through the FastAPI app in-process (no network hop), including retrieval, hydration, summary and logging.

## Answers to the research questions (interpretation of the tables above)
- RQ1: yes against BM25 - S2 is better than B0 on every ranking metric and the randomization test gives a small p-value. S2 and S1 are close on D1 (no significant difference); the hybrid mainly helps conceptual queries and recall, and on D2.
- RQ3: keyword queries are easy for all systems; **paraphrased queries are where BM25 collapses** (everyday words vs legal language) and where dense retrieval helps most; conceptual queries benefit from fusion.
- RQ4: B0 cannot retrieve Italian text from English queries; the multilingual dense model can. English queries are, however, those most often wrongly flagged as insufficient evidence (see error analysis).

## Error analysis (S2, test split)
Failures listed by `eval/error_analysis.py` in `eval/results/failures_latest.json`. **Categories were assigned by the LLM that built the system, by reading the top-3 passages; the student must review them.**

| Category | Queries | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| A. Vocabulary mismatch (everyday vs legal wording) | Q004, Q013, Q019, Q021, Q023, Q030, Q036, Q056 | The answer uses legal terms ("controllo a distanza", "preavviso di rigetto", "ritorsione", "falsa attestazione della presenza") absent from the query; BM25 matches misleading words (e.g. "da casa" -> "lavoro a domicilio", "cartellino" -> "cartello") and the dense model ranks related but wrong articles. |
| B. Right document, wrong section | Q041, Q049 | The correct internal document is in the top 3, but the passage is a neighbouring section (e.g. "Chiusura estiva" instead of "Orario"), so the citation is not precise. |
| C. Incomplete judgments (probably correct answers counted as wrong) | Q022, Q028, Q047 | The top passages contain an equivalent answer elsewhere (CAD art. 3-bis on domicilio digitale; L. 90/2024 art. 24 with the same 24-hour rule; D.P.R. 445/2000 art. 53 on protocol registration) but only the annotated article is in the qrels. |
| D. Threshold too strict for English queries | Q038, Q054, Q060 | Retrieval is right (relevant passage at rank 1-2) but the top dense similarity of English queries is slightly lower than for Italian ones and falls just under the threshold calibrated on a mostly Italian tuning set. |
| E. Out-of-scope query not flagged | Q062, Q069 | Strong partial overlap: "parcheggio dipendenti" matches "mezzo proprio" travel rules; "dipendenti di Ente Demo" matches the internal code of conduct. |
| F. Questionable annotation | Q065 | Labelled out of scope, but L. 104/1992 does mention access to "asili nido" for children with disabilities: the corpus partially answers it. |
| G. Ambiguous query | Q015 | "chiedere dati" is read as the data-subject right of access (privacy) rather than civic access to public documents. |
| H. Corpus noise (secondary factor) | Q038, Q054, Q060 | Lists of cooperatives and a page with shifted-font text from the Gazzetta Ufficiale issues enter the top 3 for English queries. |

Implications (future work, not done): add a cross-encoder reranker (category A, B); calibrate thresholds per language or on a larger tuning set (D); pool and judge the top-10 of all systems to complete qrels (C); strip non-target acts from GU issues (H).

## Validation before automation (shadow mode)
Implemented: with `DISPLAY_SYSTEM` and `SHADOW_SYSTEM` (default S2 shown, B0 shadow; the validation configuration in the protocol is B0 shown, S2 shadow) every search logs both rankings in `shadow_log`, and users' feedback is stored. What has NOT been done: a real shadow phase with real users of a real organization (no real organization; OPEN_QUESTIONS Q7). The offline evaluation above is the only evidence; it is a simulation of the process.
