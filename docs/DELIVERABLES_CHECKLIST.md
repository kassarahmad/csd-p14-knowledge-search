## Project-sheet items not (fully) covered
- "Quality of returned sources" as a human-judged rubric: not measured (needs human evaluation).
- "Reduce the time needed to find information": measured only through proxies (latency, MRR).
- "Connect relevant sources on the same topic": only through mixed results; the `/related` endpoint is a stretch goal.
- Website/intranet and shared mailboxes as sources: out of scope (DATA.md, ADR-003).
- Reranking: not implemented (stretch goal S3).

## Before submission (student)
- [ ] Review the LLM-generated material (ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md section 5; D1b documents; error-analysis categories).
- [ ] Ask the professor the open questions (OPEN_QUESTIONS.md, at least Q1, Q4, Q7, Q11-Q13).
- [ ] Re-run `scripts/reproduce.py --clean` after any change and check `eval/check_doc_numbers.py` returns 0 problems.
